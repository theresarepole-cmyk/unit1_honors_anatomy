Medical Terminology

Break terms into prefixes, roots/combining forms, and suffixes. Use those meanings to infer the whole term.

Directional Terms

Use superior/inferior, anterior/posterior, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, and superficial/deep.

Structural Organization

Know the six levels from chemical through organism, and distinguish anatomy from physiology.

Planes & Cavities

Quadrants & Regions

Application

Directional Terms Challenge

The forehead is ____ to the chin.

Answer: Superior

The heart is ____ to the skin.

Answer: Deep

The fingers are ____ to the elbow.

Answer: Distal

The nose is ____ to the cheeks.

Answer: Medial

The shoulder blades are ____ to the chest.

Answer: Posterior

In anatomical position, the thumb is on which side of the hand?

Answer: Lateral

A person lying face upward is in what position?

Answer: Supine

Practice Quiz

Open each answer after making your choice. This version does not depend on JavaScript.

1. Which word part means inflammation?

A. -itis
B. -ectomy
C. -logy
D. supra-
Check answer

-itis means inflammation.

2. The fingers are farther from the shoulder than the elbow. Which term best describes the fingers relative to the elbow?

A. proximal
B. distal
C. medial
D. superficial
Check answer

Distal means farther from the point of attachment.

3. Which level comes immediately after tissue?

A. cell
B. organ
C. organ system
D. organism
Check answer

Organ comes after tissue.

4. Studying how quickly the kidneys filter blood is primarily:

A. anatomy
B. physiology
C. directional terminology
D. body planes
Check answer

Physiology focuses on function.

5. Which system uses hormones for long-term regulation?

A. Nervous
B. Endocrine
C. Respiratory
D. Digestive
Check answer

The endocrine system uses hormones.

6. Which plane divides the body into anterior and posterior portions?

A. Sagittal
B. Midsagittal
C. Frontal / Coronal
D. Transverse
Check answer

Frontal / coronal separates anterior and posterior portions.

7. Which cavity contains the brain?

A. Thoracic
B. Cranial
C. Pelvic
D. Abdominal
Check answer

The cranial cavity contains the brain.

8. Appendix pain is most associated with which quadrant?

A. RUQ
B. LUQ
C. RLQ
D. LLQ
Check answer

Appendix pain is associated with the RLQ.

Application & Written Reasoning

Medical Terminology

Anatomical Description
